Privacy Policy

How this website handles your information.

This website is operated by Sumaya Seafood Trade International, 17/5 Sonadanga East Lane Road, Khulna 9000, Bangladesh (“we”, “us”).

What this website collects

This website does not use cookies, analytics or advertising trackers, and it does not store the information you type into the enquiry form.

Enquiries

The enquiry form does not send anything by itself. When you press “Send by email” or “Send on WhatsApp”, it opens your own email or WhatsApp app with your message filled in, and nothing is sent until you send it from there. We use the details you send us, such as your name, company, email address, phone number and message, only to answer your enquiry and to prepare quotations. We do not sell your information.

Third-party services

This website loads fonts from Google Fonts, and the contact page shows a map from Google Maps. Links to WhatsApp open WhatsApp’s own service. These providers may receive technical information such as your IP address under their own privacy policies.

Contact

For any question about your information, write to info@sumayaseafoodbd.com.
