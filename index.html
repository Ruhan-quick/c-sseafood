04 Quality control

Checked at the factory, tested in the lab, inspected before it ships.

We have established a thorough quality control system that records when and where ingredients and products were caught and how they were processed.

i. At the factory

Our people on the floor

We dispatch quality control staff to local factories to provide guidance and control, so that hygiene and quality standards are met. Sanitary inspections, visual inspections and sensory tests protect the product all the way to the final pack.

ii. In the lab

Verified with data

We conduct tests for microorganisms, pesticides, veterinary drugs and allergens, improve inspection systems and give technical support at production sites, and research new analytical techniques.

iii. Before shipment

Certified for export

Every consignment carries a health certificate from Fish Inspection & Quality Control (FIQC). Pre-shipment surveys by SGS, Baltic Control, Lloyd’s or Intertek are available on request.
