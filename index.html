Reading the grades

Shrimp sizes are counts per pound: 16/20 means 16 to 20 shrimp to the pound. A uniformity of 1.25 means the largest 10% of shrimp in a unit weigh no more than 1.25 times the smallest 10%.

What we offer.

04 Quality

Checked against your specification before it ships.

We agree the species, size grades, forms and packing with you in writing, and check the order against them before it leaves Bangladesh.

i. During production

Followed to the pack

We follow each order through production with the processing plant, so the product, grades and packing match what you ordered.

ii. For export

Health certificate

Seafood exports from Bangladesh carry a health certificate from Fish Inspection & Quality Control (FIQC), issued after bacteriological and residue testing.

iii. On request

Independent inspection

Pre-shipment inspection by SGS, Baltic Control, Lloyd’s or Intertek can be arranged at your request.
