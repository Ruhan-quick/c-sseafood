Quality Policy

Quality control and follow-up

Food is a source of health. Nowadays, interest in food safety and security is increasing. For this reason, Sumaya Seafood is working to ensure safety.

We have established a thorough quality control system that records, for example, when and where the ingredients and products were caught and how they were processed. We also consider this a responsibility to our society.

We called on processors and group companies, our main business partners, to establish a QC Cooperation Committee to ensure strict quality control and safety: a “safe and secure” management system including information exchange among members of the cooperative association, training and study sessions for quality management practitioners, building a management system, and checking production sites.

We dispatch quality control staff to local factories to provide guidance and control, so that hygiene and quality standards are complied with. We have established a final product check system for safe and reliable use, which protects deliciousness by conducting sanitary inspections, visual inspections and sensory tests to ensure thoroughness. To make this know-how visible to customers and to raise our staff’s awareness of quality, we have obtained external certification for the quality management system required at each site. Furthermore, we are working on higher quality assurance, such as unique quality standards for each item and careful follow-up.

Our inspection system

Sumaya Seafood has established the Food Safety team as a department responsible for safety assurance based on scientific evidence. It verifies the safety of all products with scientific data and maintains the quality assurance capability of the entire group. The following activities are undertaken to improve this:

  • Conducting various tests for microorganisms, pesticides, veterinary drugs, allergens, etc.
  • Improving the inspection system and giving technical support at production sites
  • Research, dissemination and development of new analytical techniques

To verify product safety, we have established a system that can perform various tests on microorganisms, pesticides, veterinary drugs, allergens and more. In addition to inspecting products delivered directly to customers, we inspect the raw materials used from the planning and development stages of products, to ensure stable product quality.

Microorganism and pesticide tests have been certified to the international standard ISO/IEC 17025, ensuring the reliability of analysis at a global standard level.

Government quality policy

Fish Inspection and Quality Control (FIQC), the local government sanitary and health authority of Bangladesh, provides the health certificate, which confirms that the products have passed all kinds of bacteriological tests and are ready for export. The fields of analysis are:

  • TPC
  • Coliform
  • Faecal coliform
  • Salmonella
  • Melanosis
  • Vibrio cholerae
  • Chloramphenicol
  • Nitrofuran metabolites
  • Tetracycline
  • Malachite green
  • Crystal violet

Third-party inspection

Pre-shipment inspection and survey by SGS, Baltic Control, Lloyd’s or Intertek, at the request of clients.

Pre-shipment inspection

Sumaya Seafood has its own quality and pre-shipment inspection policy and strictly maintains it, which assures the whole process of an order from production to shipment. Before shipment, we randomly check each order for:

  • Freshness
  • Quality
  • Net weight
  • Count
  • Packing
  • Labelling

We also suggest a container loading plan according to the client’s needs.
