Quality

Food safety matters to every buyer, and to the people who eat what we ship.

On this page

  • Your specification
  • Government health certificate
  • Third-party inspection
  • Pre-shipment checks

Your specification

Every order starts with a written specification: species, size grades, forms and packing.

We follow the order through production with the processing plant and check it against that specification before it ships. If anything is unclear, we ask before production starts, not after.

Government health certificate

Fish Inspection and Quality Control (FIQC), Bangladesh’s sanitary and health authority for fish exports, issues the health certificate confirming that products have passed bacteriological and residue testing and are ready for export. The fields of analysis include:

  • TPC
  • Coliform
  • Faecal coliform
  • Salmonella
  • Melanosis
  • Vibrio cholerae
  • Chloramphenicol
  • Nitrofuran metabolites
  • Tetracycline
  • Malachite green
  • Crystal violet

Third-party inspection

Pre-shipment inspection and survey by SGS, Baltic Control, Lloyd’s or Intertek can be arranged at the buyer’s request.

Pre-shipment checks

Before shipment, we check each order for:

  • Freshness
  • Quality
  • Net weight
  • Count
  • Packing
  • Labelling

We can also suggest a container loading plan to suit your needs.
