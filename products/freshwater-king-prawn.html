Freshwater King Prawn

Macrobrachium rosenbergii

Freshwater king prawn, also known as the Giant River Prawn, Giant Freshwater King Prawn or Scampi, is one of the biggest freshwater prawns in the world. It is widely cultivated in several countries and is popular for its value as a food source.

Mostly farm raised, scampi is meaty and moderately tender, light in colour, and has a more pronounced flavour than other species.

  • Farm raised
  • Raw & cooked
  • Block, semi-IQF & IQF
  • Sizes 2/4 – 31/40
