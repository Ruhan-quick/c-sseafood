Black Tiger Shrimp

Penaeus monodon

Black Tiger shrimp is commonly known as the Giant Tiger Prawn, Jumbo Tiger Prawn or Asian Tiger shrimp. Striped like its jungle namesake, it is meaty, juicy, firm-textured and mild in flavour. The Black Tiger shrimp is available year-round and is one of Asia’s major aquaculture products; in general, it is the most prominent farmed crustacean product on the international market.

  • Farm raised
  • Raw & cooked
  • Block, semi-IQF & IQF
