Harina Brown Shrimp

Metapenaeus monoceros

A medium-sized shrimp. Harina shrimp make up an important portion of crustaceans and have an important commercial value in the aquaculture industry. This shrimp is meaty, firm-textured and mild in flavour.

  • Farm raised
  • Raw & cooked
  • Block, semi-IQF & IQF
  • Sizes 41/50 – 100/200
