Cat Tiger Shrimp

Penaeus semisulcatus

A medium-sized marine shrimp. Cat Tiger is a marine species also called cocktail shrimp or flower shrimp. This shrimp is meaty, firm-textured and mild in flavour.

  • Farm raised
  • Raw & cooked
  • Block, semi-IQF & IQF
  • Sizes 41/50 – 300/500
