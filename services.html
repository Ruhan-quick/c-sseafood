We are professional

Sumaya Seafood started its operation as a seafood trading and buying agency, established in 1997 in Bangladesh, with offices in London, USA, Thailand, Singapore and Malaysia.

Our intention is to cater to all your shrimp and seafood needs by sourcing and supplying the finest quality products from Bangladesh to our respected global clients.

What we offer

We offer a system of professional services which can cover all the jobs that a sourcing agent should do: helping you find the best deal from a reliable supplier, following up your orders, controlling quality, and making sure there is no delay to your shipment.

Why choose us

  • Expert in seafood sourcing and inspection
  • We offer a reasonable price for better service
  • Best deal from reliable suppliers
  • Strong quality control procedures
  • Save cost & time

Our service includes

  • Sourcing all kinds of seafood products from Bangladesh
  • Finding a reliable and competitively priced supplier
  • Negotiating the price on behalf of clients
  • Confirming assortments according to clients’ demand
  • Strictly supervising and monitoring every process from production to shipment
  • Pre-shipment inspection
  • Confirming on-time shipment
  • Assuring commitment according to contract
  • Negotiating a flexible payment system for regular and exclusive customers
  • Maintaining correspondence and feedback about market behaviour, and providing transparent information to clients and packers
  • Providing all kinds of support to our valued customers, and to exporters as well

Our working method

Minimum order quantity
1 × 20ft FCL
Terms of delivery

Terms of payment
LC or T.T

What can we do for you?

Our goal is to supply you shrimps with standards and to offer a smooth and professional cooperation. We do this by working closely with an extended network of reliable processing factories and partners in Bangladesh, while having our own quality control people to guarantee our commitment on quality.
