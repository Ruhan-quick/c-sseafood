About Raisa Global Trading

Exporters of premium seafood and fresh produce, based in Dhaka, Bangladesh.

Raisa Global Trading is a Dhaka-based exporter of premium seafood and fresh produce from Bangladesh. Our seafood specialities are scampi, Black Tiger shrimp, crab and eel, and we supply the wider Bangladeshi catch on request.

We also deliver fresh vegetables and fruits, from gourds, beans and green chilli to mango, jackfruit and litchi, so buyers can source both from one partner in Bangladesh.

What we export

  • Scampi — Freshwater king prawn, head-on to butterfly cut
  • Black Tiger Shrimp — Farm raised, raw & cooked, 2/4 – 51/60
  • Crab — Live mud crab and soft-shell crab
  • Eel — Part of our white fish range
  • Vegetables — Gourds, beans, okra, green chilli and more
  • Fruits — Mango, jackfruit, litchi, lime and more

Fruits and vegetables

Alongside seafood, we deliver fresh vegetables and fruits from Bangladesh: gourds, beans, okra, green chilli, taro and potato, and fruits such as mango, jackfruit, litchi and lime. Tell us what you need, in what quantity and where, and we will come back with a quotation.
